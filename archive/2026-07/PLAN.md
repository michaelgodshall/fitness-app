# Plan: Fitness App Revival — Product Foundation (AI-Native Family Coach + Competition Arc)

_Locked via grill — by Claude (Fable 5) + Michael Godshall — 2026-07-06_

## Goal

Revive the father-son fitness project as an **AI-native family training coach**: a product that generates every co-training session for a family — bodyweight-pure in v1 (pull-up bar included) — inside founder-validated constraints (45-minute hard cap, movement-pattern balance, progressive density, strategic repeats for personal-best moments), so it **never exhausts its content** and **adapts to real family life** (who's training tonight, how much time you actually have). Determinism is preserved exactly where comparability and trust matter: monthly Trials, Challenges, and skill-progression standards are fixed. An asynchronous competition layer arrives in Phase 2 to drive teen engagement and community growth, with family-vs-family divisions in Phase 3 using improvement-based scoring. Brand and distribution lead with the authentic father-son story (intentional-fathers and church communities); the product schema is parent-child-generic. Ambition: path to primary business, gated by honest, numeric validation — first the founder's own family this summer, then 10–15 beta families, then public.

## Approach

1. **Phase 0 — Family Gate (summer 2026).** Build the core adaptive loop: session generation from "who's in / how much time" → per-member logging + difficulty swaps mid-workout → PB detection ("beat last Tuesday") → weekly Pact tracking. Plus **skills v0**: 5 hand-authored progression paths (pull-up — the universal on-ramp that muscle-up's entry gate depends on and the younger kids' only live rung — plus muscle-up, handstand push-up, pistol squat, front lever) with the "rung unlocked" feedback moment and a thin Family Record Book list (explicitly budgeted — unlock permanence is the hypothesis under test). Exercise/block library is founder-curated with LLM-assisted batch authoring (founder reviews everything before it enters the library). **Data boundary:** anonymous cloud session state on Convex (per ADR-001) — no auth, no payments, no store submission; **data-minimized, founder-family only** (nicknames, no accounts, no contact info — the Phase 1 data map must explicitly cover IPs, session tokens, and server/Convex/hosting logs, since COPPA treats persistent identifiers as personal information); slices join via room-code links; the display device owns in-flight session state (offline-resilient). **Scope cut line:** if the build isn't family-usable by mid-August, cut in this order — phone controllers (fall back to single-device log strip; **async slices then complete on the shared family device, which still satisfies the non-sync gate**) → Ghost Bar/Secret Assignment → coach voice beyond templates → Torch Board lineage (keep the bare Record Book list) → Front Lever path, then Pistol (never Pull-Up — it's the on-ramp).
2. **Gate 0→1 (fully operationalized).** Over 4+ consecutive weeks: (a) **3+ sessions/week, of which ≥2 are synchronous shared sessions** (≥20 min, ≥2 members incl. an adult) — micro-sessions and solo slices fill the remainder only; (b) **voluntary return:** per-session attendance-friction log (willing / pushed / refused) shows ≤20% "pushed" and zero sustained refusal in weeks 3-4; (c) **teen engagement (numeric, from skill-progressions.md):** the 14-year-old views his skill ladder outside sessions ≥1×/week AND self-declares ≥1 rung test per 2 weeks, sustained across the window; (d) one deliberately **non-synchronous week** (simulating fall schedules) with its own gate pattern that *replaces* the ≥2-synchronous rule for that week only: no synchronous session required; every member completes ≥2 solo slices, the family logs ≥3 session-equivalents, and the Pact machinery holds. A miss triggers diagnose-and-fix, not advancement. Calendar constraint: family training on the engine **before summer ends**. Async slice-completion is a Phase 0/1 substrate: synchronous sessions remain the flagship; slices are the school-year pressure valve. **Week-2 kill-switch:** before UI investment, the family runs planner-generated sessions from a plain screen/printout — if generated sessions don't excite the family on paper, stop and rethink.
3. **Phase 1 — Beta (fall 2026).** Accounts + family model: under-13s are parent-owned sub-profiles (nickname + birth year only, no child login). **COPPA compliance gate before recruiting families with minors:** privacy data map (incl. persistent identifiers on child-facing surfaces), vendor DPA checklist, analytics policy (none on controller surfaces), parental review/delete flows, counsel or safe-harbor review. **Youth-S&C sign-off** on the H11 constraint layer before beta. Build: monthly Trials (fixed 5-test battery; first Trials = calibration baseline, mints no Progress Points), Challenges, onboarding, coach voice v1, **both-parent monthly recap**. Recruit **10–15 families** via intentional-father/church communities and r/daddit (≤⅓ founder-close relationships); beta is free; founding families get the founding-year price honored (see step 5 — no lifetime promises). Recruitment materials A/B "adaptive coach" vs "AI coach" framing. Naming/brand sprint completes before recruitment. Recon before public build-out: incumbent family features (Apple Fitness+/Peloton/Nike/Fitbit) **plus a substitute map** (YouTube + whiteboard "dad does it manually," youth sports conditioning, homeschool PE, AI workout generators); competitor teardowns repeat at each phase boundary.
4. **Gate 1→2 (numeric, dropout-honest).** Denominator = all recruited families (dropouts count as failures): ≥60% at 3+ sessions/week for 6 weeks AND **≥8 retained families** in absolute terms, segmented by family shape. **WTP is proven by payment, not interviews:** ≥5 retained beta families pay (or place deposits at) the founding-year price (~$79/yr, year one — not locked-forever) when invited. **Gate 1.5 (cold-channel):** ≥25 **qualified** non-network waitlist signups (qualified = verified email + target-family fit (parent with kids 8-14) + explicit beta interest + stated 3×/week willingness) or ≥3 cold-recruited beta families before any public-launch spend — warm-community traction alone does not validate acquisition. **Billing exists before public:** the paid founding cohort runs privately on Stripe + the server-side entitlements table as Phase 1 exit work, so payment-proven WTP precedes Phase 2, not follows it.
5. **Phase 2 — Public v1 (winter 2026–27).** **Monetization is sequenced, not pre-committed** (per monetization research): free beta → capped, time-boxed **paid founding cohort** (~$79/yr founding-year price, not locked-forever) as the real WTP instrument → permanent model chosen from own retention data (annual-led trial vs. generous freemium both live; the RevenueCat community-growth carve-out plausibly favors a free tier for a referral-driven family product). A **sponsored-family path** stays open throughout (mission: accessible to dads/families; the product is also a consulting-funnel asset). Asynchronous competition seeds: skill milestones and improvement boards — **percentiles hidden until per-test/cohort N thresholds are met** (own-history and private community cohorts first); competition stays low-stakes, family-level, opt-in until verification standards exist. **Teen companion access** (13+, parent-granted, own consent design): solo sessions log into the family system and count toward the family Pact.
6. **Phase 3 — Deepening.** Family-vs-family divisions once liquidity exists, scored on **improvement vs. own baseline** (a fairness *hypothesis* — calibration baselines, per-test caps, participation floors; league math validated before it ships). Runtime LLM session generation as the engine upgrade. Dumbbell/hybrid programs (the teen demand acknowledged and deferred). Broader-audience marketing beyond the wedge.
7. **Cross-cutting: platform/stack decided — ADR-001 (ratified 2026-07-06).** Web-first: one web app serving the big-screen shared display + phone controllers (QR/room-code join, no installs, no child credentials); Convex backend + Clerk auth (Phase 1+); big-screen-authoritative sync (display owns in-flight state, phones are optimistic loggers); Expo native + store presence in Phase 2 via shared pure-TS domain packages; Stripe-primary billing with a server-side entitlements table. Reversibility hedges and watch items in the ADR. Big-screen-primary remains a Phase 0 *hypothesis* — the same web app serves a propped tablet, so the A/B is free.
8. **Working model.** 8–10 protected founder-hours/week (planning floor that survives busy client months); milestone-gated roadmap, not calendar-gated (single exception in Gate 0→1); AI agents execute between founder sessions; foundation docs in `docs/` govern all future sessions.

## Key decisions & tradeoffs

- **AI-native engine over authored programs.** Solves the two failure modes the founder's family proved (content exhaustion after completing Xero twice; consistency collapse under busyness) at the cost of build complexity and generated-content trust risk. Chosen over the "authored spine" because a solo founder authoring all-age progressions forever is a capacity wall and collides with path-to-primary-business.
- **Engine architecture: deterministic constraint-based planner + LLM at authoring time + LLM coach voice.** No LLM call between the family and tonight's workout: sessions are assembled by unit-testable planner code from a founder-reviewed block library; the LLM generates library candidates in batches (founder approves) and powers the coaching voice — where "voice" means Tier 1 templated lines (LLM-drafted at authoring time, offline slot-fill at runtime) plus Tier 2 async artifacts with fact-traceability; never a live in-session LLM (resolution per docs/design/coach-voice.md §6). Trades maximum adaptivity for offline capability, near-zero runtime cost, and quality one person can eval. **Runtime LLM generation is an explicit Phase 3 upgrade**, not a vague maybe.
- **Determinism where comparability matters.** Trials, Challenges, and skill standards are fixed even inside a generative product — benchmarks must be stable to mean anything.
- **Bodyweight-pure v1, pull-up bar included.** Defends the relative-loading scaling magic and the brand promise ("one program, all ages"). Weights arrive in Phase 3, not v1.
- **Father-son wedge in brand/distribution; parent-child-generic product.** No gender in the data model (legacy onboarding already asked "what do you want to be called — Dad, Mom, Coach…"). Marketing dollars chase dads in year one; nothing excludes anyone.
- **Skills v0 ships inside Phase 0** as instrumentation for the teen-drift question — accepts ~1–2 weeks of added scope to make the summer gate answer all three validated failure modes (exhaustion, consistency, teen drift).
- **Shared family device only in Phases 0–1; teen companion (13+) in Phase 2.** Minimizes early COPPA surface; later captures the solo-training drift the founder's son already demonstrated instead of fighting it.
- **Competition: asynchronous first, head-to-head later.** Own-history and private community cohorts first; percentiles hidden until per-test/cohort N thresholds are met; family divisions wait for critical mass (Phase 3). Family fairness via improvement-based scoring is a **fairness hypothesis** — supported by all-time-best baselines, a calibration first-Trials, per-test caps, and participation floors — with the league math to be validated before Phase 3, not assumed.
- **Monetization: sequenced, not pre-committed** (per monetization research + Codex round 1): free beta → capped paid founding cohort (~$79 founding-year price, never "lifetime") as the payment-proven WTP instrument → permanent model (annual-led trial vs. generous freemium) chosen from own retention data at the Phase 2 boundary. Sponsored-family path stays open throughout (mission + consulting-funnel value).
- **Platform: web-first per ADR-001 (RATIFIED 2026-07-06).** One web app = big-screen shared display + phone controllers (QR/room-code, no installs); Convex + Clerk; big-screen-authoritative sync; Expo native in Phase 2 via pure-TS domain packages; PWA-first (2025 plan) and native-first both rejected in the ADR. Big-screen-primary remains a Phase 0 hypothesis the same build tests for free.
- **Validation gates are numeric and written.** Misses trigger diagnosis, not silent advancement. This plan's predecessor died of unvalidated momentum; this one is gated.

## Risks / open questions

- **Form factor** — big-screen-primary + phone-as-controller remains a hypothesis (research found only analogies — Jackbox, one academic prototype — not commercial fitness evidence); Phase 0 A/Bs it in-family for free (laptop display vs. tablet propped vs. controllers on/off).
- **Stack/backend** — decided (ADR-001) with written reversibility hedges: pure-TS domain packages, Phase 2 Postgres-fallback benchmark; Convex company-health is a watch item. COPPA data map and consent flows land as a Phase 1 gate.
- **Monetization** — research complete; model is sequenced (see Key Decisions); the permanent trial-vs-freemium call is deliberately deferred until own retention data exists.
- **Engine quality** — a badly generated session burns family trust; mitigations: founder-reviewed batches only, planner unit tests, eval rigor as an explicit Phase 0 deliverable, family gate before any external user.
- **Demand** — the white space is now populated (RollStrong, Fitly converging on the positioning); the shared-session mechanic is unproven in-market either way. Beta voice-of-customer + incumbent recon are Phase 1 deliverables. Speed matters more than it did in 2025.
- **Dyad lifecycle churn** — kids age out; teen path + Phase 3 weights arc mitigate; model honestly at Phase 2.
- **Solo-founder capacity** — if protected hours fall below ~8/week for a sustained month, cut scope at the next gate rather than silently stretching the timeline.
- **Naming/brand identity** — unresolved; naming sprint scheduled before Phase 1 recruitment.

## Out of scope (through Phase 2)

Wearables/health-data integrations; computer-vision form feedback; nutrition; social feeds/chat; user-generated programs; non-English localization; native TV apps (casting/AirPlay acceptable as the cheap big-screen path); weights/dumbbell programs; mom/daughter-targeted marketing (product supports, marketing later); real-time head-to-head battles; multi-device sync beyond in-room session controllers, async slice-completion, and teen companion access; runtime LLM session generation.
