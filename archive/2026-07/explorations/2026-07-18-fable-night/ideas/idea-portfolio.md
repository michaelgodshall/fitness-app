# Idea Portfolio — 2026-07-18 Fable Night

Sorted by judgment call, not source-doc order. "Cost" is build effort (S/M/L). "Showcase" = client-demo value.

## Worth a founder look this quarter

| Idea | One-liner | Phase | Cost | Showcase | Devil's-advocate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Comeback, honored | Celebrate the return from a lapse as its own honored stat/ritual, never quantify the absence | 1 | S | N/A | Risks normalizing lapsing; must never measure the gap, only the return |
| Sealed Letter From This Year | Coach writes a fact-traceable year-end letter per member from the log, sealed and reopened next season | 1 | S | High | Emotional bar is brutal — a flat letter is worse than none; needs founder taste-pass every time |
| Why Tonight Looks Like This | Narrates existing planner Reason records into a 3-sentence weekly parent note | 1 (v1-adjacent) | S | High | Can become The Academic — keep to 3 sentences, opt-in, kill if ignored twice |
| Torch Pass Prophecy | Projects a hedged date a kid crosses Dad's record, from real trend data | 1 | M | High | A missed projection breeds disappointment; must stay wide-banded and parent-only pre-crossing |
| Founding Day re-enactment | Annual re-run of Placement Day battery, shows full-year delta | 1 | S | N/A | Does nothing for month-2 collapse; thin marginal value over Rematch Night — fold in, don't ship standalone |

**Why these five:** all Phase-1/S-M, all reuse existing plumbing (Reason records, Tier-2 async pipeline, Reset Rep/Rematch Night), none touch a new fence, and each targets a named failure mode (consistency collapse, teen drift, or parent-payer felt-value) directly rather than decoratively.
