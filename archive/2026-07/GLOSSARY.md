# Family Co-Training Coach

The ubiquitous language for the family fitness coach: generated co-training sessions for a parent and kids on a shared screen. This glossary governs all specs and docs — when a doc and this file disagree on a term, fix the doc. (Retro-built 2026-07-16 from the 2026-07-06 foundation session; terms marked ⏳ await founder ratification of the decision-brief rulings.)

## Language

### People & structure

**Family**:
The account-holding unit — one parent-owned account containing all members.
_Avoid_: team (legacy), household, group

**Member**:
One person in a family, adult or child, with their own targets, bests, and skill state.
_Avoid_: user, player, participant

**Sub-profile**:
An under-13 member: nickname and birth year only, no login, no contact info, owned by the parent account.
_Avoid_: child account, kid login

**Teen companion**:
A 13+ member's parent-granted access on their own device (Phase 2); their solo training feeds the family system.

**The coach**:
The product's voice — unnamed by design; its authority comes from the program and the family's own record.
_Avoid_: AI, assistant, bot, any proper name

**Judge**:
The member holding the official counter or stopwatch during a Trials test; the count is the judge's. Role rotates per test. ⏳

**Squad**:
A group of 4-6 families sharing a consistency surface (Phase 2 community mechanic). Reserved word — don't use for anything else.

### Sessions & training

**Session**:
One planned training event, generated by the planner for tonight's roster and time budget.
_Avoid_: workout (legacy docs; acceptable in coach speech, never in specs)

**Roster**:
The members actually training tonight — the planner's first input.

**Time dial**:
The declared minutes available tonight (15-45); the planner's second input.

**Block**:
A typed unit of work inside a session — straight sets, superset, circuit, AMRAP, for-time.

**Benchmark block**:
A block whose exact structure recurs on a planner-scheduled cadence so "beat last time" comparisons stay honest. The only block-level source of PBs.
_Avoid_: workout PB (legacy concept it replaces)

**Swap**:
One member substituting an exercise along its chain (easier / lateral / harder) without changing the shared session. Private request, public result.

**Swap chain**:
The ordered ladder of variants of one movement, from easiest to hardest rung of difficulty.

**Pattern**:
A movement category the planner balances across the week (horizontal/vertical push and pull, squat, hinge, core, conditioning).

**Micro-session**:
A session under 15 minutes; still credits the Pact (capped at one credit per member per day).

**Slice**:
One member's scaled portion of a shared session, completed solo on any device; settles into the parent session's history.
_Avoid_: makeup, catch-up workout, solo session (different concept)

**Solo session**:
A session planned for one member from scratch — not derived from a missed shared session (that's a slice).

**The Reveal**:
The moment tonight's generated session materializes on the big screen — the product's opening handshake.

**Tomorrow's Card**:
A one-tap reserve at session close ("same time Thursday?") that pre-commits the next session's roster and window. ⏳

**Reset Rep**:
The deliberately short, easy, PB-free session the planner generates after a lapse — the return workout is the intervention.

**Absence Ledger**:
The skip-reason tags (sick / travel / homework / tired) the coach visibly learns from.

**Placement Day**:
The first family session: bar audit plus rung placements for every member, one page of output.

### Consistency (the Pact)

**Pact**:
The per-member weekly session commitment — the product's retention core.

**Member line**:
One member's weekly Pact progress against their own target.

**Family ring**:
The aggregate Pact surface; the family's week completes when every member line completes.

**Streak**:
Consecutive complete family weeks. Resets without shame; Reset Rep fires instead.

**Covered session**:
A planned session in which every intended participant completed their portion — synchronously or by slice — within the week.
_Avoid_: session-equivalent (gate jargon; "covered" is the concept)

**Grace weekend**:
The weekend following Trials week during which a missed Trials still counts, permanently flagged "late." Always available. ⏳

### Benchmarks & scoring

**Trials**:
The fixed monthly 5-test battery — the sacred, never-varying benchmark. Splittable across up to 3 sessions within Trials week.

**Calibration Trials**:
A family's first Trials: baseline only, mints no Progress Points.

**Challenge**:
An earned, fixed max-effort event, unlocked by completing 6 sessions. (Pending founder decision: system renames to **The Crucible**.) ⏳

**Co-op Challenge**:
A Challenge format with one shared work pool the whole family burns down together (e.g., the Family 500), tag in/out freely.

**Capacity**:
The absolute scoring axis: each member's universal tier per test. Never age-adjusted.

**Tier**:
A universal threshold band per test — Rookie, Trainee, Skilled, Advanced, Elite, Champion. Fixed forever.

**Progress Points (PP)**:
The improvement axis and family headline: minted when a member sets an all-time best or unlocks a rung, per a published stepped table. The future league currency. ⏳ (table shape)
_Avoid_: XP, score, coins

**All-time best**:
A member's best-ever result on a Trials test or benchmark block; the only baseline PP mints against.
_Avoid_: record, high score, PR (use PB in UI copy)

**PB Watch**:
The shared-screen state entered when a member is within 90% of a relevant all-time best; visibility is age-gated (under-11 private by default).

**Trials Report**:
The coach-written monthly artifact: per-member deltas, tier moves, Progress Points, one highlighted moment.

**Highlighted moment**:
The report's single featured story, selected by a published merit heuristic (firsts > tier crossings > margin) with a drought guard. ⏳

**Record Book**:
The permanent family ledger — bests, rung unlocks, milestones. Writing to it is **the Chalking**.

**Torch Board**:
Record lineage inside the Record Book: current holder, past holders, and the **torch pass** when a kid finally out-does a parent.

### Skills

**Skill path**:
A named progression toward a calisthenics skill — Pull-Up, Muscle-Up, Handstand Push-Up, Pistol Squat, Front Lever.

**Rung**:
One stage of a path with a binary, parent-judgeable pass standard. Fixed forever; ladders extend upward, never revise downward.
_Avoid_: level, step, stage

**Rung test**:
A declared, witnessed, fresh attempt at a rung's standard — one attempt per declaration.

**Witness**:
The parent who observes and validates a rung test; the witness is the universal verification gate.

**Gap meter**:
The always-visible distance between a member's best practice result and the next rung's standard.

**Earned third slot**:
A third active skill path, unlocked by standard (complete a path, or hold rung 5+ on two) — for anyone, any age. ⏳

**Park-bar rung**:
A rung requiring a full-height bar (doorway bars lack overhead clearance) — framed as an outing, not a blocker.

### Surfaces & engine

**Big screen**:
The shared family display (laptop, monitor, cast tab) — authoritative for the in-flight session; never touched mid-workout.

**Controller**:
A member's phone joined to the session by room code; an optimistic logger, never required.

**Room code**:
The session-scoped join code (QR / 4 letters); controller identity is per-session — no credentials ever live on a kid's device.

**Log strip**:
The single-device logging affordance (one prefilled row per member) — the degraded mode that must stay first-class.

**Planner**:
The deterministic session generator: same inputs and seed, same session. Operates inside hard constraints; never improvises.
_Avoid_: AI (in specs), generator

**Capability state**:
The coach's memory of one member on one pattern — where they work, how they're trending — updated only from logged training, never hand-set.

**Library**:
The founder-approved content the planner draws from: exercises, blocks, coach lines. Nothing enters without founder review.
