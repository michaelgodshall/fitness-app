# Founder Decision Briefs — 18 Questions, Judged

_2026-07-07. Method: 6 Fable advocates (3 domain bundles × 2 opposing sides), each arguing to win with the opposition steelmanned; judged by the main-loop instance holding the full session context. Full advocate briefs preserved in the session scratchpad (debate-a1/a2/b1/b2/c1/c2.md). Each ruling below is a **recommendation** — mark ✅ accept / ❌ veto / ✏️ annotate. Anything accepted gets folded into the governing docs; anything vetoed reverts to the doc's prior state or your annotation._

**Judging posture, disclosed:** ties broke toward (1) your lived failure modes over theoretical purity, (2) safety-by-structure on youth questions, (3) the Phase 0 gate's instrument integrity, (4) published tables over formulas (church-picnic legibility), (5) reversible-cheap over irreversible-expensive.

## Bundle A — Trials & scoring

### Q1 · Trials judging — RULING: one official counter, **judge role rotates per test**
**Crux:** authority-inversion magic vs. record integrity + idle kids.
Simplicity's best point: dual-count structurally revokes the 8-year-old's authority — "his taps are the record" dies the moment a reconciliation audit exists. Richness's best point: 5 tests × 1 judge leaves most of the family as "the crowd," and a Trials miscount is uncorrectable downstream by design.
**Ruling:** one counter keeps the magic; **rotation** (everyone judges at least one test) answers the idle-kid problem without diluting authority; the existing confirm/accept step + full-screen public taps is the audit layer. Confidence: high. **Flips if:** observed count disputes in your family's first two Trials.

### Q4 · Progress Points economy — RULING: **published stepped table — no formulas**
**Crux:** mid-ceremony predictability vs. pricing the hard stuff honestly.
Simplicity's best: the mint is live arithmetic on the wall — a kid must be able to predict his points before the attempt; margin-scaling is incommensurable across reps/seconds and pays most for *stale baselines* (anti-consistency). Richness's best: flat pricing tells your 14-year-old his front-lever months are worth a rung-1 dead hang, and dad's economy flatlines (Principle 9 violated for the person paying).
**Ruling:** a synthesis both briefs half-conceded: **flat points per PB event + tier-weighted climb bonuses (higher tiers pay more — dad's rare Champion-line rep registers) + skill-rung mints stepped by stage tier (early/mid/terminal — the teen's terminal months pay most).** Every number is a table lookup a 9-year-old can read; zero continuous formulas; the table is published in the Record Book like tier thresholds. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** the teen audibly dismisses flat PB mints, or a family shape is systematically favored in Phase 3 league modeling.

### Q5 · Co-op pool formula — RULING: **age-band quota v1; capped capacity-hybrid as the designed Phase 2 upgrade**
**Crux:** "age is a proxy for capacity the product already measures" vs. comparability + coupling debt.
Richness's best: quotas band your 9- and 10-year-olds identically despite a year of training difference. Simplicity's best: co-op is **self-balancing by construction** (tag in/out freely — strong members take more of the pool; the family races the cap and its own history), you must ship the age-band fallback anyway (first Trials = calibration-only), and coupling debt is this doc-set's documented #1 failure mode (40 findings).
**Ruling:** published age-band quota for v1; pre-register Richness's own fallback — **age-band base ± capacity adjustment capped at ±15%, Trials-snapshot-frozen** — as the Phase 2 upgrade if quota pools observably mis-size. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** pools consistently finish far under cap with no felt struggle AND racing prior times fails to restore intensity (both conditions).

### Q6 · Grace weekend — RULING: **always available**, flagged late, telemetry-watched
**Crux:** valve reliability vs. ritual scarcity + periodization.
Scarcity's best (technical): the Trials cycle is the engine's *only* deload wave — a slippable date desyncs the taper. Always's best (values + data): busyness *clusters* — quarterly rationing reinstates the legacy wall on the second busy month, hitting families exactly when the promise ("bends to your life") matters most; 16 months of lived data sits behind this failure mode, and the "late" flag already prices lateness honestly.
**Ruling:** always available. The masking objection dissolves (the late flag IS the collapse signal — telemetry reads late-rate either way), and taper retargeting by ≤7 days is an engine feature we control — build it cheap. Adopt Scarcity's monitor: if >⅓ of a family's Trials land on grace across two quarters, the coach addresses it as a consistency conversation (Pact jurisdiction), never a lockout. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** Phase 1 telemetry shows grace becoming the norm *with* declining ceremony engagement.

### Q15 · Trials-report highlight — RULING: **earned via published heuristic, engine-proposed, founder-confirmed, hard drought guard**
**Crux:** scheduled fairness vs. honest selection.
Round-robin's best: an "earned" spotlight is member-vs-member editorial in the product's most permanent artifact, and founder-tunable makes Dad the visible favoritism target. Earned's best: per-member coverage is *already guaranteed* by the report's mechanical check (everyone gets a named moment monthly), and a rotation-forced highlight on a flat month is the Amnesiac anti-pattern on a schedule — the doc's own golden line is merit-picked.
**Ruling:** engine proposes from a **published** hierarchy (firsts > tier crossings > largest tier-weighted margin — firsts structurally favor the young kids), founder confirms in the existing review flow, and the drought monitor is hardened to a **constraint**: no member goes 2 consecutive months unhighlighted (auto-overrides to their best moment). Dad's fingerprints stay off it — the published heuristic picks; he just approves the render. Confidence: medium. **Flips if:** any kid shows distress over selection, or the monthly confirm becomes a chore you skip.

## Bundle B — Skills & youth safety

### Q9 · First muscle-up standard — RULING: **hip-drive counts, with a codified binary check + "The Strict Muscle-Up" pre-registered as the next rung from day one**
**Crux:** the window vs. the standard's purity — both sides argued from the doc's own rules.
Standards' best: "controlled hip drive" is a judgment call on a momentum spectrum, violating the binary-judgeability rule on the system's most important test. Momentum's best: the doc concedes strict may sit beyond the summer window ("a rung six months away kills the mechanic" — for the gate-critical member), and in real calisthenics culture a clean controlled bar muscle-up *is* a muscle-up — the strict version is a separately named harder skill.
**Ruling:** hip-drive counts, and Momentum's rebuttal supplied the missing binary: **"feet never swing forward past the bar's plane before the pull" — side-video judged.** That satisfies the judgeability rule better than "zero momentum" (itself a purity spectrum). Pre-registering **The Strict Muscle-Up as the visible next rung from day one** kills the "discount version" objection — the ladder extends upward, never revises downward. Confidence: high. **Flips if:** Placement Day shows the teen at/past Strict 10 with sternum-height pulls — strict is then in-window and this question dissolves.

### Q10 · Front lever, under-10s — RULING: **hard pause at Advanced Tuck, printed on the ladder from day one**
**Crux:** the one path where safety-by-construction inverts.
Momentum's best: the pause almost never binds in Phase 0 (entry requires Strict-3 pull-ups + three rungs of climbing) and "passed but locked" corrodes the ladder contract. Standards' best (the sharpest safety reasoning in the debate): lever physics hand *light kids* the strength standard early, on the tissue least ready — the prerequisite-gate philosophy fails on exactly this path, and symptom-gating trusts a 9-year-old's self-report under sibling pressure, the signal every other subsystem refuses to trust.
**Ruling:** hard pause — and the ladder-lies objection is answered by *honest printing*: the age/clearance condition appears on the ladder from day one, exactly like the park-bar condition on muscle-up rungs (a stated standard, not a lie). Symptom rule + volume cap retained for everyone. The pause self-expires on birthdays and converts into anticipation. Safety asymmetry decides ties here. Confidence: high. **Flips if:** the pre-Phase-1 youth-S&C review explicitly clears capped straight-arm work for under-10s.

### Q11 · Skill-test attempts — RULING: **attempt-liberal in Phase 0; pre-registered 48h post-fail cooldown as the Phase 1 fallback**
**Crux:** the gate's instrument vs. ceremony decay.
Cadence's best: declared tests are the system's most max-effort events and its only ungoverned intensity channel. Liberal's best: you cannot measure unprompted engagement while throttling it (the pre-mortem ratified this exact principle), and the witnessed-test protocol already rate-limits — a parent must convene, one attempt per declaration.
**Ruling:** liberal through Phase 0 (the founder is in the room for every attempt — parent judgment beats a timer); if ceremony decay or frustration-grinding is *observed*, the fallback is a 48h post-fail cooldown, stated openly, not a silent scheduler. Beta families (Phase 1) revisit with thinner supervision assumed. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** a kid burns multiple weekly declarations on the same rung with mounting public frustration.

### Q12 · Active-path cap — RULING: **2 default; a third slot EARNED by standard — universal rule, no age exception**
**Crux:** frequency math vs. the teen's park-gated empty Tuesdays.
Cap-2's best: the doc's own math can't feed three paths at 2-3 practice sessions/week each. Cap-3's best: muscle-up rungs 3-6 are park-only, so "3 active" is functionally ~2 home-active — the third path back-fills the weekday vacancy the other rules dug.
**Ruling:** the synthesis both briefs converged toward — **complete a path, or hold rung 5+ on two paths, and a third slot unlocks. For anyone.** It's the house philosophy verbatim (prerequisite gates, not age gates), it answers the sibling with a standard instead of a birthday, and your 14-year-old likely earns it on Placement Day (Strict 10 completes the Pull-Up path instantly). Strictly better than either assigned position. Confidence: high. **Flips if:** nothing — this one's clean; tune the earn-condition ⚙ if needed.

### Q7 + Q8 · Day-one protocol — RULING: **run "Placement Day" — merged protocol**
Both advocates proposed compatible 30-minute protocols; the merge takes Standards' ordering discipline (bar audit first; **no straight-arm or front-lever testing day one**; Pistol staged last so the kids out-placing Dad closes the day) plus Momentum's mechanics (max 3 attempts/member/path; one side-video take; the teen's chest-to-bar attempt IS the clearance verdict; **calendar the first park outing before the event ends**; Dad places under the same standards). One-page record: bar line, per-member rung+value+witness, enrollments, park decision, teen-reaction sentence, ⚙ flags. This is your first founder task when the app can log it — or run it on paper sooner and the data seeds calibration.

## Bundle C — Session UX & product

### Q2 · Set-block advancement — RULING: **auto-advance at rest+10s (as ratified), with the human accelerator: all-logged → screen goes loud → any member taps to advance early**
**Crux:** dead air vs. kinetic shame.
Tap-side's best: the wall visibly moving past the unlogged 9-year-old is tenet 4's ban rendered as motion, and it compounds silently. Auto-side's best: the floor fires only after *programmed rest* + 10s — everyone was scheduled to be done; a member still under load that late means targets were mis-set (a different bug), and the doc already ratified this sentence.
**Ruling:** keep the ratified backstop, adopt the tap as the *primary* path (when everyone logs early, any member advances the room — same empowerment as any-member start). Session zero explicitly watches the youngest for rushed-feeling; the floor is ⚙. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** the floor repeatedly fires mid-set for the youngest — retune the floor, don't remove the backstop.

### Q3 · Lobby re-roll — RULING: **no re-roll in Phase 0; one constrained re-roll ships in Phase 1**
**Crux:** planner trust + gate data vs. the flat-night rescue.
No-side's best: in Phase 0 a flat reveal is a *planner defect the founder must feel* — a re-roll converts engine bugs into invisible slot-pulls, corrupting the gate's instrument while the founder is the only person who can fix the engine. One-side's best: boredom is your #1 lived failure mode, swaps can't fix "tonight's draw is flat *for us*," and the re-roll rate is exactly the library-breadth sensor Phase 1 recruitment needs.
**Ruling:** phase-split. Phase 0: no button — flat nights go in the friction log and drive library authoring. Phase 1: **one re-roll/session, no preview, benchmark/skill anchors pinned across draws** — it becomes both the valve and the sensor precisely when the founder leaves the room. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** Phase 0 sessions are abandoned at the Reveal at a meaningful rate after library fixes — then the button arrives early.

### Q13 · Coach identity — RULING: **unnamed "the coach" through Phase 1; leave room for family folklore**
**Crux:** relationship warmth vs. persona debt.
Named's best: families will name it themselves anyway, surrendering register control. Unnamed's best: a name invites the chat/backstory expectations Tier 3 deliberately doesn't exist to meet, and warmth in this product comes from *specificity, not identity* — knowing Leo's incline work earned him full push-ups bonds regardless of the speaker's label.
**Ruling:** unnamed — and the "they'll name it themselves" point is a *feature*: earned folklore ("the boys call it Sarge") beats assigned branding, costs nothing, and can be harvested at the Phase 2 teen-companion review with the product brand finally locked. Naming the coach before the product has a name is backwards. Confidence: high. **Flips if:** your family stably names it and unnamed references cause real awkwardness — then adopt *their* name.

### Q14 · Big-screen audio — RULING: **author the starter set audio-capable now; ship pre-rendered spoken lines as a cut-eligible Phase 0 item; never live browser TTS**
**Crux:** the room's physics vs. brand-quality control.
Earcon-side's best: web TTS hands the brand's voice to an unreviewable renderer, and a garbled safety line is the worst possible failure. Spoken-side's best: mid-plank nobody reads — the mid-set lines' channel contradicts their content as text — and the architectural trap is real: a text-only line library must be reopened line-by-line to add audio later.
**Ruling:** the pre-render move dissolves the quality objection (one chosen voice, rendered offline, founder-auditioned like every other asset, zero device variance, offline-safe). So: **tags + length budgets now** (cheap, irreversible-to-skip), **pre-rendered audio for the small hand-authored starter set** in Phase 0, sitting on the cut line (eval zero kills a cringe voice in week one). Safety lines: text + pre-rendered only, never synthesized. Live TTS: never. Confidence: medium-high. **Flips if:** the family prefers silence or the 14-year-old cringes — the tags survive for Phase 2 regardless.

### Q17 · Tomorrow's Card + Session Authorship Token — RULING: **Card stays in Phase 0, respecced; Token defers to Phase 1**
**Crux:** consistency armor vs. the scope wall.
Defer-side's best (a genuine catch): the Card as drafted is *circular* in Phase 0 — no push channel exists until Phase 2, so a night-before reserve is seen only by someone who already opened the app. Keep-side's best: the mechanism lives in the *shared surface* (the kid who saw tomorrow's card holds the family to it), and the Card is the Absence Ledger's cleanest skip-detector (open finding F30).
**Ruling:** the circularity dissolves with a respec: **the reserve prompt fires at session close** — the family is already in the app, at the calm moment ("same time Thursday?"). One tap, feeds pre-generation and the Absence Ledger, no push needed. The Token defers: its agency job is now covered by the earned-third-path mechanic (Q12) plus liberal testing (Q11), and it's the most build-expensive of the extras. Its Phase 1 slot is bought by evidence if the teen's engagement sags despite the other hooks. Confidence: medium-high.

### Q18 · Family Meter — RULING: **hold stands + run the cheap experiment now**
**Crux:** the hold's experiment can't produce its own evidence (a good summer proves nothing about a bad November) vs. surface dilution.
**Ruling:** keep the hold, adopt Promote-side's instrument: **one plain-text line on the session close — "Family total: 12,400 reps since June"** — an hour of work, no meter visual, no new surface. If the kids' eyes snag on it across Phase 0, the Meter earns a conditional Phase 1 slot (recap-priority, cut-eligible); if it lands flat, the hold was right and we know it. Confidence: medium.
